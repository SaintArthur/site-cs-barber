Scope: the CS Barber landing page (index.html). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: the barbershop owner, on a phone, between clients, arriving from a
WhatsApp link. Job: decide whether this system is worth a conversation. Secondary
audiences named by the user: the barber who has to live in the tool, and the end client
who receives the confirmation and the reminder. Action: fill the qualifying form, which
assembles a WhatsApp message. Proof on hand: real panel screenshots and seven real FAQ
answers. No metric, no customer count, no testimonial exists; none may be invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the shop adapts nothing — the system is assembled around how this barbershop
already works. The page refuses the arrangement this category always ships, a feature
grid of sixteen equal tiles that asks the reader to do the sorting; here the modules
arrive in two tiers, what every shop gets on day one and what it turns on later, so the
reader sees a starting point instead of a catalogue. User took the standing exit: this is
the category convention played straight, with Booksy Biz, AppBarber and Trinks as the
craft bar.

OWN-WORLD: the incumbent brand carries over — near-black navy ground (#0a0d14), cream
text, a single gold accent (#c9a56a) that is spent only on the primary action and on the
act numerals, Poppins throughout. Components: flat cards with a one-pixel line, no
gradient fills, radii at 6px for controls and 10px for cards, generous vertical rhythm,
Section headings stand alone and carry their own weight: no eyebrow, no kicker and no
ordinal above them. Ordinals appear only in the four implantation steps, where the
sequence is the information the reader needs. Recognizable with all content removed by:
the single gold spent only on the primary action, the full-bleed hairline that opens each
section, and the two-tier module block.

STORY: the visitor understands that their shop's mess has a shape and a name; believes
that a system built in-house and assembled per shop fits where off-the-shelf does not;
and fills the form to start the 7-day trial.

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed near-black. Fixed header, 64px, logo left, four links and a
gold pill CTA right; on a phone, logo and burger. Headline left-aligned, three lines at
clamp(32px, 8vw, 60px), cream, tight leading. One supporting paragraph at 17px, max 36rem.
Primary gold CTA and a glass secondary CTA side by side below it, stacked under 480px.
A four-up strip of plain facts under a hairline: 7 dias, 1 painel, Assistida, Seg a sáb.
To the right on desktop, the real panel screenshot at 1.6:1, flat, one-pixel line, no
device chrome. On a phone the screenshot sits below the facts. Primary action is visible
without scrolling at 390x844.

FORM: the category canon, taken as the standing exit at the user's explicit request. No
concept-seed roll was run and no seed key exists: the user named the destination and the
three products that set its bar before any round opened, which is the exit the direction
round exists to offer. Ordered alternatives not pursued: the pinned WebGL film already
built and live, which the user is replacing.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
